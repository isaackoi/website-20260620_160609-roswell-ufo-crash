## Introduction

Roswell’s [tourism]({{ 'tourism/' | relative_url }}) culture succeeds because it does not require visitors to agree about what happened in 1947. Some arrive convinced that an extraterrestrial craft crashed near the town. Others accept the U.S. military explanation or remain unconvinced by UFO claims altogether. Many are simply curious travellers looking for an unusual roadside attraction. Yet all of these groups can participate in the same museums, festivals, shops, and public spaces. Roswell has developed a tourism model in which belief, scepticism, humour, and local identity coexist rather than compete. The result is a destination where disagreement is not a problem to be solved but part of the attraction itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/southeast/roswell-ufo-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newmexico.org">[New Mexico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newmexico.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">New Mexico Roswell, NM International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center Museum exhibits include information on the Roswell Incident, crop circl</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New MexicoRoswell, NM International UFO Museum &amp; Research CenterMuseum exhibits include information on the Roswell Incident, crop circles...</span></span></span>

## How One Town Serves Different Visitor Motives

One reason Roswell remains popular is that visitors often arrive with very different expectations.

For committed UFO believers, Roswell is a place of pilgrimage. The International [UFO Museum]({{ 'ufo-museum/' | relative_url }}) presents witness accounts, documents, and competing interpretations of the Roswell incident, giving enthusiasts material to study and discuss. Some visitors spend extended periods researching in the museum library, treating the site as more than a novelty attraction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">International UFO Museum and Research Center &#124; Gift Shop...Come and explore our exhibits, witness firsthand accounts, an...</span></span></span>

For sceptics, the appeal is often different. They may come to examine the claims for themselves, to understand why the story became so influential, or simply to experience a famous piece of American folklore. The museum itself explicitly states that its exhibits are not designed to force visitors toward a particular conclusion and instead encourage questions. That approach allows sceptical visitors to engage without feeling that they are entering a space that demands belief.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/southeast/roswell-ufo-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newmexico.org">[New Mexico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newmexico.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">New Mexico Roswell, NM International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center Museum exhibits include information on the Roswell Incident, crop circl</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New MexicoRoswell, NM International UFO Museum &amp; Research CenterMuseum exhibits include information on the Roswell Incident, crop circles...</span></span></span>

A third group consists of tourists who are largely indifferent to the factual debate. For them, Roswell is an entertaining cultural destination. Alien-themed streetscapes, souvenir shops, festivals, costumes, and themed restaurants provide a distinctive travel experience regardless of one's [views]({{ 'views/' | relative_url }}) on extraterrestrial life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.istockphoto.com/photos/roswell-ufo-new-mexico-famous-place" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: istockphoto.com">[iStockPhoto.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">istockphoto.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">90+ Roswell Ufo New Mexico Famous Place Stock Photos...Alien street lights line the downtown streets of Roswell, New Mexico.The small so...</span></span></span>

The key point is that Roswell does not market itself solely as a place where aliens definitely crashed. It markets itself as the place where the question remains interesting.

## Why Ambiguity Is Useful for Tourism

Many tourist destinations depend on certainty. Historical sites often present a settled narrative about what happened and why it matters. Roswell operates differently.

The unresolved nature of the Roswell story creates a broad audience. If definitive proof of either an alien spacecraft or an ordinary weather-related explanation had universally convinced the public decades ago, the mystery might have faded. Instead, uncertainty keeps the story alive. Believers can interpret Roswell as evidence of a [cover-up]({{ 'cover-up/' | relative_url }}), while sceptics can view it as a fascinating example of myth-making, [media]({{ 'media/' | relative_url }}) influence, and conspiracy culture. Both perspectives provide reasons to visit.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roswell_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 6, 2026 — The town of Roswell promotes itself as a destination for UFO-associated tourism.... ## Where Humour and Seriousness Coexist

One of Roswell's most distinctive features is the way serious investigation and playful entertainment occupy the same space.

Within a short walk, visitors may encounter exhibits discussing witness [testimony]({{ 'testimony/' | relative_url }}), military documents, and historical controversies alongside gift shops selling alien figurines, novelty clothing, and cartoon extraterrestrials. Festival schedules often combine lectures by UFO researchers with costume contests, family activities, and themed entertainment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">International UFO Museum and Research Center &#124; Gift Shop...Come and explore our exhibits, witness firsthand accounts, an...</span></span></span>

This combination might appear contradictory, but it broadens Roswell's appeal. A visitor can spend part of the day examining competing interpretations of the 1947 incident and another part posing for photographs with oversized alien statues. The town does not force a choice between seriousness and fun.

Observers of the annual UFO Festival have repeatedly noted this mixture. Festival programming includes speakers, authors, and discussions for enthusiasts, while simultaneously offering parades, costumes, and light-hearted public events. Even promotional materials explicitly invite both believers and sceptics to participate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newmexico.org/events/summer-events/roswell-ufo-festival/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newmexico.org">[New Mexico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newmexico.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">roswell ufo festival</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New MexicoRoswell UFO FestivalRoswell UFO Festival July Fourth Weekend Roswell, NM (575) 914-8018 enjoying live entertainment, family-fri...</span></span></span>

The coexistence of these tones lowers the social cost of participation. Someone who is uncertain about UFO claims can attend without being seen as endorsing them. Likewise, believers can enjoy the festive atmosphere without abandoning their conviction that the underlying mystery deserves serious attention.

## Shared Participation Matters More Than Shared Belief

The economic success of Roswell's UFO tourism demonstrates that a shared experience can matter more than a shared conclusion.

The International UFO Museum attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors over time and more than 220,000 annually according to museum officials, while UFO festivals have drawn thousands of attendees and generated significant economic impacts for the city. These numbers are unlikely to be composed solely of dedicated UFO believers.
